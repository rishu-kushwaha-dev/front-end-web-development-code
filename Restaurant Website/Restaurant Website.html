Our Story

Crafted with Passion, Served with Soul

At Shree Hotel & Restaurant, every plate tells a story. We source the finest Indian spices and ingredients and transform them into dishes that celebrate both tradition and innovation.

Our open kitchen invites you to witness the artistry behind each creation, while our warm, modern space sets the stage for unforgettable evenings.

  • Authentic Indian Spices

    Hand-picked spices sourced from across India.

  • Tandoor Cooking

    Clay oven techniques for deep, smoky flavor.

  • Handcrafted Everything

    From fresh naan to house-made desserts, made in-house.
